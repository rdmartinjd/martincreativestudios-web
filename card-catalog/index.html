Books, music, movies & comics

One personal catalog, with tailored details for each kind of media. Keep your own ratings, notes, and collection information.

Scan, search, or add

Scan a barcode on iPhone or iPad, search for a title, or add it yourself. Look up available details and covers, then make the record your own.

🔍

Lists that work your way

Create hand-picked lists or Smart Lists that update from your rules. Keep a reading queue, favorite films, or records to revisit.

📖

Keep track of lending

Record who has a borrowed book, movie, album, or comic. Keep lending details alongside the rest of the item’s history.

Across your Apple devices

Use iCloud to keep your personal catalog across iPhone, iPad, and Mac. No separate Card Catalog account or studio-operated library server.

📤

Import, export, and back up

Bring in supported library files and CSVs. Export CSV or HTML, print a collection, and use JSON backup and restore for supported details and artwork.
